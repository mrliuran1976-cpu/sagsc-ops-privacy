SAGSC Ops Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-01

SAGSC Ops is an internal warehouse operations app used only by authorized staff of our company. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Who can use the app

The app is not available to the general public. Accounts are created by the company for its staff; there is no public sign-up.

Information we handle

  • Account information: username, display name and an optional profile photo, used to sign in and identify the operator.
  • Work photos: photos taken with the camera or chosen from the photo library to document warehouse operations (for example container check-in, loading and special instructions).
  • Operation records: barcodes scanned, operations performed, the operator and the time, used for warehouse records and traceability.

Camera and photo library

The camera is used to take work photos and to scan container, pallet and waybill barcodes. The photo library is used only when you choose to attach an existing photo or set your profile photo. The app does not record audio.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect location data, contacts or health data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your information with third parties.

Storage and retention

Information is sent over the network to the company's own warehouse management servers and kept only as long as needed for business records. Access is limited to authorized staff.

Your choices

You can change your profile photo in the app at any time. To correct or delete your account information, contact your administrator or us at the address below.
